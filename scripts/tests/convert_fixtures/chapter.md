# Field Study

This chapter reports the **field study**: where the samples came from, how
they were measured, and what changed between the *two seasons* [@lamport1994].

## Sites

The three sites differ in soil, slope, and water. Site C had a 15% slope and
the wettest ground; about 1/3 of its plots flooded in spring.

![Map of the three sites](figures/sites.png)

| Site | Plots | Slope (%) | Soil |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| A | 12 | 2 | Sandy loam |
| B | 18 | 6 | Clay & silt |
| C | 9 | 15 | Peat |

## Methods

Each plot was sampled twice, as in earlier work [@lamport1994]. The mean mass
was $\bar{m} = 12.5$ g with σ ≈ 0.8 g.

$$
\bar{m} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} m_i
$$

### Instruments

1. A balance with 0.01 g steps
2. A soil probe
   - set to 10 cm
   - cleaned after each plot

![Photo of the probe](figures/probe.png)

## Results

Yield rose at every site; site C changed the most.

Table 2: Yield by site and season

| Site | Spring | Autumn |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| A | 4.2 | 4.6 |
| B | 3.9 | 4.4 |
| C | 2.1 | 3.3 |

> Field notes: the autumn samples at site C were taken after rain.
