## RPI's requirements and original template

The formatting rules come from RPI's Office of Graduate Education (OGE): the
*Dissertation and Thesis Manual* and checklists on the
[OGE submission page](https://graduate.rpi.edu/academic-progress/submit-your-thesis-dissertation).
The class was also compared with RPI's official LaTeX template, available
from RPI ITS's
[Thesis Preparation page](https://itssc.rpi.edu/hc/en-us/articles/360010087512-Thesis-Preparation),
whose files credit Harriet Borton (ITS) and Michael Sofka. This project is an
independent implementation: it copies none of RPI's files and does not
include the RPI seal, which each student downloads from RPI.

## What's ours and what isn't

| Whose | What |
| --- | --- |
| Ours (the people above) | The class (`rpi-thesis.cls`), starter examples, scripts, guide, diagrams, cheat sheet, and website, under the [MIT license](LICENSE.md) |
| RPI's | The requirements, manual, checklists, original LaTeX template, seal, and name; not covered by this project's license |
| Other projects' | The tools and fonts below, under their own licenses |
| Yours | Your thesis or dissertation: your text, figures, data, and permissions; never covered by this project's license |

The full record, with sources and how each item is treated, is the
[attribution record](docs/project/attribution.md).

## Tools

The toolkit uses or recommends these free tools; none is bundled in the starter.

- [LaTeX](https://www.latex-project.org/) and its packages from
  [CTAN](https://ctan.org/), including
  [biblatex](https://ctan.org/pkg/biblatex), with
  [Biber](https://ctan.org/pkg/biber) and
  [latexmk](https://ctan.org/pkg/latexmk), as distributed in
  [TeX Live](https://tug.org/texlive/)
- [Pandoc](https://pandoc.org/), which builds this website and the offline guide
- [pdf.js](https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js), by Mozilla and contributors
  (Apache License 2.0), which the website's optional "Check your PDF" page
  includes unchanged to read a PDF in your browser
- [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/) and
  [Better BibTeX for Zotero](https://retorque.re/zotero-better-bibtex/)
- [LaTeX Workshop](https://github.com/James-Yu/LaTeX-Workshop) for
  [VS Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/)

## Add or update a credit

When feedback, a tip, a test, an example, or a fix is used, the maintainer
asks whether its contributor would like to appear in the table above, and
adds their chosen public name and a short description of the help. You can
opt in at any time, change how you are named, or ask to be removed: say so
on an issue, or through the form linked from
[ask for help](docs/troubleshooting.md#ask-for-help).
Anonymous help is welcome. Do not publish an email address, student number, or inferred
identity, and do not turn issue authors or commit metadata into a list of
people automatically. Ways to help are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
