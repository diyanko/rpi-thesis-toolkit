RPI Thesis Toolkit
Unofficial

You do not need to know LaTeX.

Writing an RPI master's thesis or doctoral dissertation? Write it in plain words. A free starter sets up the margins, spacing, front pages, and page numbers the way the OGE manual describes.

Most of what you type looks like this:

\chapter{Introduction}
Rivers shape the towns near them.
\section{Earlier work}
As \textcite{ortiz2021} found,
a chapter your words a section a citation

Three ways in

  1. 1
    Open in OverleafWrite in your browser. Nothing to install.
  2. 2
    Download the starterFiles for your own computer, with the guide inside.
  3. 3
    Already have a LaTeX thesis?Add only the class file; the guide lists what to change.

Free tools in your browser

  • Settings builderA form for your title page.
  • Convert to LaTeXFrom Word or Google Docs.
  • Check your referencesFinds common problems.
  • Explain my errorPlain words for an error.
  • Check your PDFMeasures margins and more.
  • Nothing you paste is uploaded. They run on your own computer.

Tell me where you got stuck.

Hi! I made the RPI Thesis Toolkit so that formatting your thesis takes less of your time, and less of your worry, than writing it.

It only gets better when people tell me what went wrong. You do not need to be good at LaTeX to help. A first try that went sideways is the most useful thing you can share.

  • Where did you get stuck?
  • What did you not like?
  • What do you wish it had?
  • What would make your life easier?
