For RPI theses and dissertations

The RPI parts are set up. Fill in your details and write.

  • Title page: your name, degree, and committee, typed once

  • References: one style throughout, straight from Zotero

  • Contents and lists: made for you

  • Published chapters: a ready place for the attribution

What you do

  1. 1. Write anywhere

    Word, Google Docs, Markdown, a notebook.

  2. 2. Paste into Convert to LaTeX

    A first pass; read it over.

  3. 3. Compile

    Every piece in place, in order.

  • Change one piece

    Move a chapter, redo a plot, or switch citation style; the numbering and references follow.

  • Stuck? Use the tools

    Explain my error, Check your references, and Check your PDF run in your browser.
