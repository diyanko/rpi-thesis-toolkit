Tools

Run in your browser

  • Settings builder Fill in a form; copy your title page settings.

  • Bring your work in Paste from Word or Google Docs; get LaTeX.

  • Check your references Finds common problems in references.bib.

  • Explain my error Paste an error; see what it means.

  • Check your PDF Measures margins, page numbers, and contents.
