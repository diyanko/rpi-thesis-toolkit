$-- ============ Your first hour ============

Then

Your first steps

Three small steps once you have the files and LaTeX is installed. Get started walks through each. Already writing? Jump to what you need.

  1. 1

    Compile once

    Open main.tex and compile it (turn your files into a PDF) before you change anything.

    You should see the sample PDF. Then add the RPI seal.

  2. 2

    Make it yours

    In metadata.tex, replace the value after each =; keep the braces and commas.

    author = {Your full name},

    Compile again; check the title page.

  3. 3

    Try a citation

    The starter already includes a source. Add its key to a paragraph and compile.

    \cite{lamport1994}

    Try the sample, then add your own sources.
