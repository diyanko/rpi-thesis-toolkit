$-- ============ Your first hour ============

Getting going

Your first hour

Four small steps, from the files to your first citation. Get started walks through each one.

  1. 1

    Get the files

    Download the starter, or upload it to Overleaf as a new project. Then add the RPI seal from RPI's site.

    Keep the folders together.

  2. 2

    Compile once

    Open main.tex and compile it before you change anything.

    You should see the sample PDF.

  3. 3

    Make it yours

    In metadata.tex, replace the words inside the braces.

    author = {Your full name},

    Compile again; check the title page.

  4. 4

    Add a citation

    Save a source with Zotero, export it to references.bib, then cite its key.

    \cite{lamport1994}

    A citation and a reference list appear.

$-- ============ Behind the scenes ============

Behind the scenes

What happens when you press Compile?

Your thesis is a handful of plain-text files. Compiling turns them into one formatted PDF, and knowing how makes error messages much less mysterious.

Draft wherever you like, typing citations as \cite{key} so they paste straight in. The formatting lives in one file, so it can be swapped when the rules change.

See how it works→
